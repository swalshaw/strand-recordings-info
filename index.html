Strand Recordings

A personal setup using rclone to store recordings in my Google Drive and access them from my home media server.

This setup is for my own use and is not an official Strand service.

Privacy policy

Google Drive access is used to upload, read, organise and delete recording files in my own account. OAuth credentials are stored on my home server and are not published on this website.

This website does not request Google sign-in or collect recording files. Recordings are not intentionally shared with third parties by this setup. Google processes stored data under its own policies.

I can revoke access through my Google Account and delete recordings from Google Drive at any time.
